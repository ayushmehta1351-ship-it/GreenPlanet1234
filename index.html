CarbonChain

"Blockchain-powered DApp for transparent, fraud-resistant carbon credit tracking with Ethereum smart contracts, preventing double-counting and ensuring immutable verification."

Problem Statement

Traditional carbon credit systems suffer from record manipulation, double-counting (1 credit claimed 3x), and unverified claims. Global market: $100B+ with 30% fraud. India's carbon market lacks transparency - no public audit trail.

Literature Review

Analyzed 20+ papers (IEEE Blockchain, Springer). Existing: Verra Registry (centralized, hackable), Gold Standard (manual audits), EU ETS (double-counting issues).

Innovation

100% immutable blockchain records • Tokenized credits (1 token = 1 MT CO2) • Smart contract automation • Real-time public verification • Zero double-counting.

Blockchain Architecture

Smart Contracts

Solidity 0.8.x • Ethereum/Polygon • Admin verification → Auto token minting • Immutable credit lifecycle

MetaMask Integration

Web3.js • Real-time balance • P2P transfers • Mumbai Testnet • Gas-optimized transactions
